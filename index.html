About me

I'm a committed advocate for an open, secure, inclusive, and trustworthy Internet, with expertise in Internet governance, digital transformation, cybersecurity, and community development. I serve as Chairperson of the Internet Society Rwanda Chapter, Coordinator of the Rwanda Safer Internet Day Committee, and Member of the Rwanda Internet Governance Forum Committee, contributing to digital policy, online safety, and meaningful stakeholder engagement. I am also an Expert Trainer at the Internet Society, delivering training in Network Operations, Computer Network Design and Deployment, Community Networks Readiness Assessment, and Encryption. I also champion Online Trust and Safety by training communities on privacy, digital footprints, and responsible digital practices, empowering individuals to navigate the digital world safely and responsibly.
