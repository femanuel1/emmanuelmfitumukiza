About Me

I am an Information Technology professional with a decade of experience spanning network operations, community-centered connectivity, Internet governance, and online trust and safety.

I currently serve as Chairperson of the Internet Society Rwanda Chapter and as a member of the Rwanda Internet Governance Forum Multistakeholder Advisory Group (RWIGF MAG). I am also a founder of the Rwanda Safer Internet Day Committee and serve as an Internet Society Foundation Learning Program Expert Trainer. Through these roles, I support capacity building in network operations, computer network design and deployment, community network readiness assessment, Internet governance, online trust and safety, and other areas of practical ICT development.

My professional work combines hands-on technical practice, teaching, capacity building, stakeholder engagement, and digital infrastructure development. I am particularly interested in applying technology to strengthen digital infrastructure, build practical ICT skills, promote safer and more trusted online environments, and expand meaningful access to the Internet. Across my technical and community-focused work, I aim to connect technology, people, and institutions to develop practical and sustainable digital solutions.

Projects

Kibungo Internet Access Center

A community connectivity initiative at GS Kibungo designed to improve access to the Internet and digital resources for learners and the surrounding community.

The project focuses on meaningful connectivity, digital inclusion, digital literacy, and access to useful online services.

Connecting GS St Tite Butereri

A school connectivity initiative aimed at improving access to digital learning resources and Internet services for students and teachers.

The project supports digital learning and helps create better opportunities for students in underserved communities.

Raspberry Pi Digital Library

A digital library initiative at GS Nyirarukobwa using Raspberry Pi technology to provide access to learning materials.

The initiative supports digital literacy, improves access to educational content, and reduces dependence on continuous Internet connectivity.

Digital Inclusion in Rural Communities

Work focused on expanding meaningful digital access in rural communities, particularly in areas such as Karongi and Nyagatare.

The work brings together community connectivity, digital literacy, online safety, and practical digital skills.

Safer Internet Initiatives

Through the Rwanda Safer Internet Day Committee, I contribute to initiatives that promote safer and more responsible use of digital technologies.

Activities include awareness and capacity building around privacy, digital footprints, online safety, responsible digital citizenship, and digital well-being.

Digital Transformation in Logistics

I have also worked with technology solutions in the logistics sector, focusing on improving operational processes and service delivery.

This includes fleet management, GPS tracking, process automation, digital logistics platforms, and better visibility of operations.
