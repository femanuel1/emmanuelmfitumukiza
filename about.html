My Profesional Profile

Home About
I am an Information Technology professional with over 10 years of experience in network operations, community-centered connectivity, Internet governance, and online trust and safety. My work focuses on strengthening digital infrastructure, advancing digital inclusion, and fostering secure, reliable, and sustainable Internet access.

I serve as Chairperson of the Internet Society Rwanda Chapter, a member of the Rwanda Internet Governance Forum Multistakeholder Advisory Group (RWIGF MAG), founder of the Rwanda Safer Internet Day Committee, and Internet Society Foundation Learning Program Expert Trainer.

Through these roles, I contribute to ICT capacity building and professional development in network operations, computer network design and deployment, community network readiness assessment, Internet governance, cybersecurity awareness, and online trust and safety. I am committed to empowering communities, strengthening technical expertise, and promoting an open, inclusive, and secure Internet that supports sustainable digital development.
